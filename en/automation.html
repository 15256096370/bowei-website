FAQ

Automation Integration FAQ

Common questions about automation retrofit and turnkey projects

What automation services does Bowei provide?
Bowei Industrial provides three types of automation services: 1) cobot integration — compact, flexible, safe robots taking over simple repetitive tasks like loading/unloading; 2) flexible manufacturing cells — integrated turnkey projects; 3) non-standard automation equipment — custom-designed for your specific processes.
What is a turnkey project?
A turnkey project means Bowei handles the full process — solution design, equipment manufacturing, installation and commissioning, operator training, production support and remote maintenance. After acceptance, the customer can put the line into production immediately without coordinating multiple suppliers.
Can existing production lines be upgraded with automation?
Yes. Bowei provides customized retrofit solutions for existing equipment — adding robots, positioning and exchange systems, and automation units without replacing whole machines, achieving economical and efficient line automation upgrades.
