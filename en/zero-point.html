ZERO-POINT POSITIONING

What Is a Zero-Point Positioning System?

A zero-point positioning system is a rapid changeover system: zero-point positioners mate with interface pallets (pull studs) so workpieces or fixtures mount with high, repeatable accuracy — no re-indicating after changeover.

In batch machining, changeover time directly drives machine utilization. Traditional setups require re-indicating and tool probing every time — slow and operator-dependent. With zero-point clamping, parts "mount and you're done", restoring position accuracy (≤ 0.003mm) immediately.

Bowei Industrial offers modular zero-point positioners + interface pallets sized to your precision and process needs, working seamlessly with exchange tables and pallet systems to build efficient flexible machining cells.

Ideal for high-precision, high-cycle machining across automotive parts, aerospace, construction machinery, medical and electronics — in both high-mix low-volume and mass production.

FAQ

Zero-Point Positioning FAQ

Common questions about zero-point system selection and use

What is a zero-point positioning system?
A zero-point positioning system is a rapid changeover clamping system. Zero-point positioners mate with interface pallets (pull studs) so workpieces or fixtures can be mounted on a machine with high, repeatable accuracy — no re-indicating or tool probing needed after changeover.
What accuracy can a zero-point positioning system achieve?
Bowei Industrial zero-point systems achieve repeatability of ≤ 0.003mm — ultra-high precision for precision machining and high-cycle lines. For lower-precision needs, the ball-lock system (< ±0.013mm) offers a more economical option.
How do I choose between zero-point and ball-lock positioning?
Zero-point offers higher precision (≤ 0.003mm) for high-precision, high-cycle machining. Ball-lock (< ±0.013mm) provides instant positioning and clamping with high rigidity at better value for medium-precision changeover. Choose by part precision and process needs — Bowei engineers can help with free selection support.
