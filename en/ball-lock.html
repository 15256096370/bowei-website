BALL-LOCK POSITIONING

What Is a Ball-Lock Positioning System?

A ball-lock positioning system is a modular rapid changeover system: ball-lock shafts mate with receiver and locating bushings to deliver instant positioning and clamping, high rigidity, and significantly reduced changeover time.

The core value of ball-lock is "insert it and it locks, locked and it's located": the instant the ball-lock shaft enters the receiver bushing, positioning and clamping complete together, with repeatability better than ±0.013mm — no extra indicating, changeover several times faster than traditional methods.

The system uses a modular design — one set needs at least 2 ball-lock shafts + 2 receiver bushings + 2 locating bushings — and can be combined freely to fit different machine tables and fixture sizes.

Ideal for medium-precision rapid changeover. Works even better combined with hydraulic fixtures and exchange tables, widely used in multi-part batch production for automotive parts and construction machinery.

Ball-Lock Positioning FAQ

Common questions about ball-lock system selection and use

What is a ball-lock positioning system?
A ball-lock positioning system is a modular rapid changeover system. Ball-lock shafts mate with receiver bushings and locating bushings to deliver instant positioning and clamping, with repeatability better than ±0.013mm and high rigidity, significantly reducing machining changeover time.
What components does a ball-lock system include?
One ball-lock system needs at least 2 ball-lock shafts + 2 receiver bushings + 2 locating bushings. The modular configuration is flexible and can be combined to fit different machine tables and fixture sizes.
What accuracy does ball-lock positioning offer? What is it best for?
Ball-lock systems offer repeatability better than ±0.013mm with instant positioning, clamping and high rigidity — ideal for medium-precision rapid changeover. If you need higher precision (≤ 0.003mm), choose the zero-point system. Bowei Industrial provides free selection support.
