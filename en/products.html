FAQ

Products & Purchasing FAQ

Common questions about Bowei products, customization and cooperation

What does Anhui Bowei Industrial do?
Anhui Bowei Industrial Technology Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise in Baohe District, Hefei, China, specializing in hydraulic workholding fixtures, positioning & exchange systems (zero-point, ball-lock, exchange tables), and non-standard automation equipment for the automotive parts, construction machinery, aerospace, medical and electronics industries — the premier workholding fixture manufacturer in East China.
Hydraulic vs manual fixtures — which is better?
Hydraulic fixtures lead on every dimension: clamp/release in 2-3 seconds; automatic clamping deviation only 1%-1.5% (manual 15%-25%); hydraulic support deviation 0.5%-0.8% (manual 25%-35%); plus automation, status monitoring and continuous follow-up clamping on raw castings. For batch machining, hydraulic fixtures clearly win on efficiency and consistency.
Does Bowei support custom fixtures? What is the process?
Yes. Bowei builds fixtures around your part drawings, process routes and machine interfaces: 1) you provide drawings and requirements; 2) solution design within 1-2 weeks with free assessment; 3) manufacturing and delivery 2-4 weeks after design approval; 4) installation, training, production support and remote service.
