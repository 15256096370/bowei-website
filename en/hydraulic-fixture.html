HYDRAULIC FIXTURE

What Is a Hydraulic Fixture?

A hydraulic fixture (hydraulic workholding) uses hydraulic oil as the power source, driving clamping elements through hydraulic cylinders to locate and clamp workpieces. Compared with manual fixtures, it delivers larger, more consistent clamping force and faster action — the core equipment for high-cycle, high-precision batch machining.

Hydraulic Fixture FAQ

Common questions about custom hydraulic fixtures

What is a hydraulic fixture?
A hydraulic fixture is a workholding device powered by hydraulic oil, using hydraulic cylinders to locate and clamp workpieces. Compared with manual fixtures, it clamps/releases in just 2-3 seconds with only 1%-1.5% automatic clamping deviation and 0.5%-0.8% hydraulic support deviation, making it ideal for high-cycle CNC machining.
How long does it take to build a custom hydraulic fixture?
Lead time depends on part complexity: typically 1-2 weeks for solution design and 2-4 weeks for manufacturing after design approval; complex multi-operation fixtures take longer. Pricing is based on fixture size, cylinder count and precision requirements. Bowei offers free solution assessment.
Which machines can your hydraulic fixtures fit? Do you export?
Bowei has built fixtures for Okuma, SW, Doosan, Mazak, Haicut and Tsugami VMCs, HMCs, twin-spindle machines and turning centers, with interfaces customized to your machine. We export worldwide with English drawings, English technical communication and remote support.
