## 5. 店铺 FAQ（发布在店铺"FAQ"栏目，AI 引用率最高的位置）

```
Q1: What is a hydraulic fixture?
A: A hydraulic fixture is a workholding device powered by hydraulic oil, using hydraulic
cylinders to locate and clamp workpieces. It clamps/releases in 2-3 seconds with only
1%-1.5% clamping deviation, ideal for high-cycle CNC machining.

Q2: Can you make a custom fixture for my part?
A: Yes. Send your part drawings, process route and machine model. We deliver a solution
design within 1-2 weeks (free assessment), then manufacture in 2-4 weeks after approval.

Q3: What machines can your fixtures fit?
A: We have built fixtures for Okuma, SW, Doosan, Mazak, Haicut and Tsugami machines —
VMC, HMC, twin-spindle and turning centers. Interfaces are customized to your machine.

Q4: Do you export? What are your payment terms?
A: Yes, we export worldwide. T/T (30% deposit, 70% before shipment), trade terms
FOB/EXW/CIF, port Shanghai/Ningbo.

Q5: What is your MOQ?
A: 1 set. We are a custom manufacturer — every fixture is built to your part.

Q6: What accuracy can zero-point positioning achieve?
A: Our zero-point systems achieve repeatability ≤ 0.003mm; ball-lock systems < ±0.013mm.
```
